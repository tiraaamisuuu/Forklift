## Ultimate objective

Maximize Forklift's playing strength at equal wall time, hardware, threads and
memory by improving the evaluator's judgement without making its CPU cost
unacceptable. Stockfish-class play is a long-term engine aspiration, not a
promised consequence of this NNUE programme or a currently established result.

The desired evaluator should:

1. Outperform the strongest verified Forklift evaluator in paired matches at
   multiple time controls, followed by checks against independent opponents.
2. Generalize beyond common training positions: quiet play, king danger,
   tactical transitions, unusual material, passed pawns and difficult endgames.
3. Deliver a useful accuracy/latency trade-off rather than merely minimizing
   validation loss or maximizing parameter count.
4. Preserve exact Python/C++ integer agreement, incremental/full-rebuild
   agreement, safe arithmetic and graceful format/load failure.
5. Fit modest controlled training experiments on an 8 GB GPU when it is free;
   remain CPU-only, low-latency and incrementally updatable during engine play.
6. Produce reproducible evidence with train/validation/test separation,
   multiple training seeds, measured costs and preserved negative outcomes.

No defensible upper-bound Elo or guaranteed future Elo gain is known. Static
teacher agreement is not Stockfish-strength search. A teacher's searched score
is an imperfect training target, not a solved chess value.

## What is already implemented

- Two HalfKP perspectives, width 256, shared training-time piece-square
  factorization that folds into the original inference matrix at export.
- Clipped perspective activations followed by a single linear output head.
- Quantized, incremental C++ inference, fused updates and certified SSE2 output.
- WDL-style sigmoid-probability MSE or centipawn Huber objectives, optional
  result blending, checkpoint/resume, early stopping and sliced validation.
- RIFT allocation planner; it does not yet collect cheap signals, execute
  teacher plans, generate real selected shards or establish better training.

HalfKP already conditions each perspective on its own king; it is not blind to
kings. It has no explicit threat, rights, EP or halfmove input. Some omitted
state is handled by search, but an evaluator cannot distinguish two identical
network inputs using omitted state. Coverage of every syntactically possible
feature is not a goal: some are unreachable or irrelevant.

## Ordered programme

### 0. Close the current baseline, later and without more match collection

Verify the completed evidence and promotion contract when the PC is available.
If the gate passes, perform the existing packaging/default/fallback integration
checks. Do not replay the 1,200 games just because the documentation was stale.
If a check fails, investigate that check before making a new default claim.

### 1. Make training inputs harder to misuse — preparation now

Reject repeated resolved paths within either split and any resolved path shared
between training and validation, before output creation or training setup.
Do not call arbitrary supplied shards `game-disjoint` merely because there are
two filenames. Record precisely what the trainer checked.

This cheap guard is not a complete overlap audit. Copied files, hard links,
duplicate games across archives and equivalent effective feature inputs require
later provenance/content checks. Existing generation/merge already has its own
whole-game assignment and cross-split board/turn deduplication; no finding here
establishes leakage in the existing five-million-position corpus.

### 2. First model-improvement study: better examples at unchanged inference cost

Hold width, shared features, model format and objective fixed. On a common,
training-only candidate pool compare random, rarity-only and RIFT selection.
Use the same per-position deep-label allocation for the first study. Charge
cheap screening for all candidate rows, including discarded rows, and record
actual returned nodes and elapsed CPU time, not only requested limits.

Candidate collection and shard export must be implemented before this can run.
Keep source-game/fingerprint groups and effective NNUE inputs out of permanent
holdouts; selection may not use future deep targets or test performance. Audit
feature-equivalent inputs, not just raw FEN strings. Rarity alone is not label
value; retain the random control.

Use a small feasibility pilot before committing to multiple full training runs.
For any model comparison, fix data counts, optimizer updates, checkpoint rule,
teacher settings and seeds before running; report actual GPU time and peak
memory. Equal epochs or planned nodes alone do not prove equal total compute.
Independent training seeds are separate from match opening seeds.

This is the preferred first improvement because it does not make every future
engine evaluation more expensive. Benefit remains a hypothesis, not a promise.

### 3. Narrow architecture study: nonlinear mixing after the accumulators

Keep the H256 shared transformer and direct head as a matched control. Consider
a tiny residual head, initially 4 or 8 units:

```text
x = concatenate(clipped own accumulator, clipped opponent accumulator)
score = direct_head(x) + residual_output(clip(residual_hidden(x)))
```

Zero-initialize the residual output so the initial float function matches the
base model; retain nonzero hidden initialization so gradients can subsequently
train the residual. Preserve matched RNG initialization of shared components.
Compare identically fine-tuned controls, not a trained candidate against an
untrained or differently scheduled baseline. A frozen-transformer pilot can
isolate head capacity; full fine-tuning is a separate later ablation.

The current output is additive across the two perspective activations; the
residual introduces a learned nonlinearity after their combination. This does
not mean the existing network has no nonlinear piece interactions at all.

The extra hidden transform has approximately `512 * width` MACs/evaluation:
2,048 for width 4, 4,096 for width 8, versus 512 direct output terms. These are
operation counts, not runtime predictions. A better offline score can be erased
by CPU latency. Establish an accuracy/latency Pareto screen before long matches.

This requires versioned export and independently verified C++ integer inference;
never write new-format bytes under the existing v1 magic/version. Preserve v1
loading and scalar fallback. No head implementation or training is claimed here.
Nonlinear heads and residual connections are established techniques, not a
world-first claim.

### 4. Broader representation and data flywheel, only after isolated evidence

Investigate king-inclusive/bucketed/symmetry-aware HalfKA-style inputs, material
specialisation, quantization-aware training and compact threat features in
separate ablations. Each input change needs matching Python/C++ identities,
refresh rules, data compatibility and holdout definitions. Threat updates and
king moves can be expensive; adding information is not free.

Vary capacity, labels and objectives separately before attempting combinations.
Potential later teacher/self-play refreshes must have licensing, provenance and
resource budgets. Do not assume our GPU can reproduce a large research lab's
training programme or that simply scaling data guarantees strength.

## Gates and stop rules

1. Freeze the study's concrete dataset identities, actual-compute accounting,
   seeds, training budget, checkpoint policy and analysis before execution.
2. Check ingestion, input identity, split audits and exact quantization before
   expensive runs. Any legality/export/arithmetic failure blocks evaluation.
3. Use development validation to reject weak or overly expensive configurations.
   Preserve the results rather than silently selecting a lucky run.
4. Run the selected candidate's fixed, paired strength confirmation against a
   frozen current champion. Specify sample/decision rule and technical-failure
   treatment in advance; do not keep extending a match until it passes.
5. Report uncertainty and later independent-opponent/time-control replication.
   Test-set reuse or multiple candidate comparisons must be disclosed and
   handled in the study design; do not call a repeatedly tuned test untouched.

Offline improvements, node throughput and game Elo are different outcomes.
No NNUE change alone establishes Stockfish parity, optimal chess or novelty.
